# Next speaking goals

Current focus: get acquainted and form a tentative picture of the learner's conversational Spanish through a natural conversation.

After the first session, choose two or three goals based on observed needs. For each, describe what the learner wants to express, where it will be useful, and what observable progress would look like.

This is a flexible guide for the tutor. The learner's topics take priority. There are no written assignments or required schedules.
